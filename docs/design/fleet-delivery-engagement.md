# Fleet-as-Delivery-Team: Client Engagement Framework

**Date:** 2026-08-24 · **Author:** Esther · **Status:** Draft v1
**Purpose:** Turn the 6-agent fleet into a sellable AI-transformation delivery
team. Real business cases → real outcomes → case studies that sell the next
client. Pilot client #1: **The Client Brand** (task 00562f6c).

---

## 1. The engagement model (5 phases)

| Phase | What | Fleet role | Client-visible artifact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P0 Discovery** | Audit current state: metrics, assets, channels, gaps; capture baselines | Esther leads; Gisu gathers channel data; Kustos data hygiene | Discovery report with baseline numbers |
| **P1 Strategy** | RICE-prioritized AI initiatives, success metrics, 90-day roadmap | Esther strategy; all agents input | AI strategy + roadmap doc |
| **P2 Build** | Ship 2-3 high-ROI capabilities | Titus build; Joseph infra; Esther QA | Working capabilities (live) |
| **P3 Operate & Measure** | Run, measure weekly, iterate | All agents on their capabilities | Weekly performance reports |
| **P4 Case Study** | Document outcomes vs baseline, testimonials, ROI | Esther authors; Kustos verifies numbers | Sellable case study + proof package |

**Rule:** every claim in P4 must trace to a P0 baseline or P3 measurement —
same honesty standard as the fleet's own reporting (no fabricated numbers).

## 3. The Client Brand pilot — the real business case

**Client snapshot:** sustainable fashion bags (Riviera Summer Tote + pouch),
Korean-American founders Amy & Luke, Seoul. ~1,400 IG followers. Tagline
"Designed with Purpose. Guided by Faith." Brand voice fully documented in the
`client-brand-brand-marketing` skill (content calendars, email sequences, copy
templates, performance framework).

**Why it works as pilot #1:**
- Real business, real P&L, real audience — outcomes are measurable
- Brand/marketing assets already exist in the skill library
- Amy + Luke are the founders — access, approvals, feedback are frictionless
- Sustainability story aligns with Esther's core identity (authentic fit)

**Planned capabilities (from the slices):**
1. **AI content engine** — brand-voice caption/reel/email pipeline (3x output)
2. **Email automation** — welcome + pre-order sequences with signup capture
3. **Analytics dashboard** — weekly performance review automation
4. **Pre-order landing page + payment** — conversion infrastructure (Joseph)

**Case-study metrics (baseline → after):** followers, engagement rate, email
signups, pre-orders/sales, content output volume, hours saved/week, cost per
acquisition.

## 4. Selling the next client

The The Client Brand case study becomes the template. Each engagement adds a new case
study to `docs/case-studies/`. The sales narrative: *"We ran a full AI
transformation for a real brand — here are the numbers. We can do the same
for you."* The fleet operating report (2026-08-24) is the capability proof;
the The Client Brand study will be the outcome proof.
