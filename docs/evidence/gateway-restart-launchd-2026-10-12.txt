1. THE TWO DEFECTS (reported by Titus, non-orchestrator macOS host, 2026-10-12)
   1a. SUPERVISION: _supervised_by_systemd() was the only check, so a launchd host
       was told 'not running under systemd' and /restart refused.
   1b. POLICY: the shipped plist's KeepAlive carried CrashedOrAbnormalExit, which
       is not a launchd condition at all. launchd.plist(5) defines SuccessfulExit,
       NetworkState, PathState, OtherJobEnabled and Crashed; for the one that
       matters here it says: 'SuccessfulExit: If true, the job will be restarted as
       long as the program exits and with an exit status of zero. If false, the job
       will be restarted in the inverse condition.'
       SOURCE (fetched 2026-10-12): https://keith.github.io/xcode-man-pages/launchd.plist.5.html
       CONSEQUENCE: a SuccessfulExit=false job is relaunched after a NON-zero exit,
       so macOS must exit 75 (EX_TEMPFAIL) — the clean exit(0) that systemd's
       Restart=always wants would PARK the job with the bot down.
