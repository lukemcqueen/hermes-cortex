Addresses the self-adversarial findings on the first close (ADV-13418-1..5), all of
one class: claims about live state need a committed, re-runnable artifact rather
than prose in an evidence file.

What was repaired: the pre-commit hook minted its task id with
`$(cmd || echo fallback)`, which captures the command's stdout AND the fallback when
the command prints and then exits non-zero. A repo with no commits makes
`git rev-parse --abbrev-ref HEAD` do exactly that (prints "HEAD", fails), so ids
like `precommit-repo-HEAD\nunknown/update` entered the ledger. The hook is fixed at
the source; this repair cleans the historical rows. Provenance is kept: each row's
own `outcome_note` appends the original, escaped, and the mapping in §3 is read back
OUT of those fields rather than recomputed.
