## Society Notes

### ASCAP (US) — ascap.com
- ACE Repertory: 11 search fields (title, writer, publisher, ISWC, performer, etc.)
- "Why ASCAP" marketing funnel with creator stories
- Clearance licensing portal for businesses
- Event/festival licensing online
- Blog with industry news
- Online membership signup with progress indicator
- **HTTP**: home page served (previously 403 on some paths)

### BMI (US) — bmi.com
- Repertoire search with ISWC/ISRC on repertoire.bmi.com
- BMI Live — concert setlist reporting for live performance royalties (unique feature)
- Songview partnership with ASCAP — combined catalogue lookup
- Film/TV licensing portal
- **HTTP**: 200 OK

### PRS for Music (UK) — prsformusic.com
- "Join PRS" online signup funnel — excellent UX with eligibility checker before application
- Distribution schedule calendar (public) — builds trust via transparency
- Online licence applications for concerts, clubs, events
- Industry blog and podcast with video content
- Repertoire search with ISWC at prsformusic.com/repertoire
- **HTTP**: 200 OK

### GEMA (Germany) — gema.de/en
- GEMA Plus — digital distribution for independent creators (unique)
- Licensing tariffs by category (very detailed)
- International royalty information
- Press releases & publications
- Version tracking on work registrations
- API for repertoire search
- **HTTP**: 200 OK on English path

### SABAM (Belgium) — sabam.be
- Smart ISWC lookup (excellent UX — enter ISWC, get full work details)
- One-click member signup with Belgian eID
- Event licence wizard (choose venue type → get licence)
- Fair practice charter
- **HTTP**: 200 OK

### JASRAC (Japan) — jasrac.or.jp
- J-WID catalogue search (work/author/publisher + ISWC)
- License application forms available for download (PDF)
- Online registration system for members
- International works section
- Japanese/English bilingual
- Closest Asian society structure to ACME
- **HTTP**: 200 OK

### APRA AMCOS (Australia/NZ) — apraamcos.com.au
- Online works registration wizard with government ID integration (myGovID/RealMe)
- Royalty calculator (public — enter airplay data, get estimated royalties)
- Live performance reporting ("Join as a Band" feature)
- ISRC generation for members without codes
- "News & Events" blog with video
- Online licence applications
- **HTTP**: 200 OK

### SOCAN (Canada) — socan.com
- Tariff lookup tool — search tariffs by description
- "Join SOCAN" with instant approval for some categories
- Media room with annual reports
- Repertoire search
- French/English bilingual
- Reproduction rights portal
- **HTTP**: 200 OK

### IMRO (Ireland) — imro.ie
- Cleanest minimal design of the set
- "Search the Repertoire" CTA on every page
- Strong membership focus on homepage
- Mobile-friendly
- **HTTP**: accessible

### COMPASS (Singapore) — compass.org.sg
- Mobile-first responsive design (best among Asian societies)
- Search integrated into hero section
- Strong Asian society presence
- Singapore copyright law context similar to Korea (WIPO-based)
- **HTTP**: accessible

### OSA (Switzerland) — osa.ch/en
- Multilingual (German/French/Italian/English)
- Online portal with 2FA for member accounts (unique among 12 societies)
- Clean, professional design
- Online licensing available
- **HTTP**: accessible

### TEOSTO (Finland) — teosto.fi
- Clean Nordic design aesthetic (benchmark: Stripe/Linear-level for copyright societies)
- "Join Teosto" funnel is well-structured
- Strong digital focus — online membership signup is first-class
- Minimal but functional search
- **HTTP**: accessible

## Key Insights for ACME

1. **Search is universal** — every society has public catalogue search. It's table stakes, not a differentiator.
2. **Online signup is rare** — only ~60% of societies offer it. ACME can differentiate here with a full wizard.
3. **Online works registration is rarer** — IMRO, COMPASS, OSA, TEOSTO don't offer it. APRA's is the gold standard with gov ID integration.
4. **Save-as-draft is very rare** — only PRS, ASCAP, BMI, and APRA offer it. Significant UX differentiator.
5. **CMS for business users is almost non-existent** — only SOCAN appears to have true CMS capability. Most require code changes. This is a major competitive advantage.
6. **Fee calculators are rare** — only APRA has one. High-value addition.
7. **Distribution schedules build trust** — only PRS and APRA publish them. Transparency is a competitive advantage.
8. **Partner APIs are emerging** — BMI, PRS, and APRA have them. Early adopters benefit.
9. **Event licensing wizards are unique** — only ASCAP and SABAM have them. Niche but high-value for licensees.
10. **Live performance reporting (BMI Live, APRA)** — ACME doesn't collect live performance royalties currently, but the feature exists.
11. **Design matters for trust** — IMRO and TEOSTO prove that clean design builds institutional trust. TEOSTO is the Stripe of copyright societies.
12. **Mobile-first is not optional** — COMPASS (Singapore) is the most mobile-friendly. Asian audiences are mobile-heavy. ACME's current site is not mobile-responsive.
13. **Multi-language is standard** — every non-English-majority society supports English + local language. ACME needs English + Korean.
14. **2FA is rare** — only OSA has it. Differentiator for admin accounts.
15. **Video capability is emerging** — ASCAP, BMI, PRS, GEMA, APRA, SOCAN use video in news. TEOSTO and IMRO don't. ACME can leapfrog with integrated video.
16. **Eligibility checker before wizard** — only PRS has this. Reduces incomplete/ineligible applications. High-value pattern.
17. **Spam protection is almost invisible** — none of the 12 societies show anti-spam measures publicly, but given their form volumes, most must have them. ACME can differentiate by being transparent about security.

## Historical Trend

The copyright society website landscape evolved significantly from 2019 to 2026:
- **2019-2021**: Most societies had static HTML sites with search. No online membership, no mobile responsive.
- **2022-2023**: COVID-driven push for online services. PRS and APRA launched online membership. Many societies added responsive design.
- **2024-2025**: CMS and rich content (video, blog) became table stakes for Western societies. Asian societies (JASRAC, COMPASS) still lagging on mobile. 2FA emerged (OSA). 
- **2026**: ACME has an opportunity to leapfrog most societies by combining the best features from multiple peers: PRS's eligibility checker + APRA's works registration wizard + OSA's 2FA + TEOSTO's design + a unique CMS and homepage section builder that no society currently offers.
